THE CLUB

The Philites were born in 2016, on the dust pitches behind Hall C at Chuka University, when a group of students from the evening philosophy discussions decided their arguments needed a scoreboard. They took one rule with them onto the pitch: think first, strike fast.

A decade later, the pickup side is Chuka University's team in the Chuka Premier League (University Division) — playing in our black-and-white striped home shirt, carrying a gold and royal blue badge. The club is student-run, coached by volunteers who never stopped loving the game, and backed by the loudest student end in the county.

Every season starts the same way: open trials, open doors — because the badge belongs to the campus.
